Knockout

Welcome to Knockout!

In this game of icy competition, the goal is simple: knock all of your opponent’s penguins off the iceberg to win!

  • You’ll play as either the dark or light penguins.
  • On each turn, drag the arrows from your penguins to set their movement direction and speed — the longer the arrow, the faster they’ll slide!
  • When you’re ready, press play. Once your opponents has as well, its time to watch the penguins launch!
  • All moves happen at once, so plan your positioning and timing carefully.
  • This continues until only one team has penguins left standing on the iceberg.

Stay sharp… or you risk the sad slide of defeat
