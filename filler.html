Filler

Welcome to Filler!

In this game, your goal is simple: capture more tiles of your colour than your opponent by the end of the game.

  • Each player starts in a corner of the board — one in the bottom-left, the other in the top-right.
  • On your turn, you’ll choose a color from the available options. Your starting tile (and any tiles you've already captured) will change to that color.
  • Any adjacent tiles (above, below, left, or right — no diagonals) that match the new color become part of your territory.
  • On your next turn, you'll expand again, capturing more matching-color tiles adjacent to any of your tiles.

The catch? You can’t choose the same color as your opponent on your turn. Use this to your advantage — smart choices can block their options!

The game ends when the entire board is filled. The player with the most tiles in their territory wins!

Good luck, and have fun filling the board!
