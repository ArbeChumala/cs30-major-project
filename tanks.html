Tanks

Welcome to Tanks!

Time to test your battle skills by destroying your opponent’s tank. You’ll play as either the blue tank or the red tank, and each of you starts with 3 lives.

When it’s your turn:
  • Adjust the turret angle by dragging the turret with your cursor.
  • Set the rocket power by dragging the power bar.
  • Once you're happy with your aim and power, press the launch button to fire!

If your rocket hits the opponent’s tank, they lose a life. Don’t fall behind — the first to lose all 3 lives loses the match!
