Reversi

Welcome to Reversi!

The goal is simple: end the game with more tiles in your color than your opponent

  • You’ll play as either black or white. On your turn, place one tile of your color on the board.
  • Your move must sandwich at least one of your opponent’s tiles in a straight line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) between the tile you placed and one of your existing tiles, with no empty spaces in between.
  • All the opponent's tiles caught in between get flipped to your color.
  • You can only place a tile if it captures at least one of your opponent’s tiles.

If your opponent has no legal moves on their turn, you get to play again. If neither of you can move, the game ends — even if the board isn’t full.

The player with the most tiles in their color at the end wins!

Tip: Corners are powerful! They can’t be flipped once captured, so try to secure them — and avoid giving them away.
