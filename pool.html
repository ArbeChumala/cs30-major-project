Pool

Welcome to Pool!

You probably already know the basics, but we’ve got a few twists — here’s how it works:

  • You’ll be assigned solids or stripes at the start of the game. Your goal is to sink all of your balls, and then sink the eight ball to win.
  • To take a shot, click and pull back on the cue, then release when you’re happy with the angle and power. Try to aim carefully — only your balls count!
  • Sink one of your balls? You get to take another shot.
  • Sink the white cue ball? That’s a foul — your opponent gets the next turn.

Once you’ve cleared all of your balls, aim for the eight ball. But be careful — if you sink it before clearing your balls, you lose the game.

Good luck!
